Sharper satellite view: the Blue Marble texture now renders at 16384x8192 (2.3x the pixels of the previous version). Coastlines, islands and ocean-floor shading stay crisp noticeably deeper when zooming in. Memory use stays around 360 MB thanks to the ShortArray palette index; devices with a large heap (such as the Fairphone 6) load it in a few seconds. The texture data files keep their ne1_50m naming for compatibility.
