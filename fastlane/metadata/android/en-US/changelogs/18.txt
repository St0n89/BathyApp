New FDTD acquisition mode: place a seismic source (depth, dominant frequency, amplitude, Hann or Ricker wavelet) and any number of receivers (depth dialog, default seafloor) on the map, load global seafloor sediment type from the GlobSed v3 dataset (5-arc-minute NOAA NCEI sediment thickness, one-time ~20 MB download, then cached offline), and export the survey as env.jl + source.jl ready to run with the FDTD2DElastic Julia suite. The export builds a 2.5-D great-circle line from the shot through the farthest receiver, sizes the grid from the dominant frequency (10 points per wavelength, automatic coarsening if needed), and assigns mud/sand/rock bottom classes with Hamilton-style geoacoustic properties from water depth and sediment thickness. Seismic credit: GlobSed v3, Olson et al. / NOAA NCEI, served via the CaltechDATA mirror.
