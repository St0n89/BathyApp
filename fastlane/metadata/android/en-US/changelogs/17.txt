New: optional HD satellite imagery on the globe (Esri World Imagery). In cities and coastal regions down to sub-metre per pixel instead of the previous NASA Blue Marble resolution; the "Satellit HD" chip fetches tiles for the visible area only on demand and caches viewed regions on the device (default off, the app stays offline-first; without network the globe automatically falls back to the built-in NASA texture). Note: while the layer is active the Esri server sees which areas are requested (same as the GEBCO online mode). Imagery credit: Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS User Community.
